Online coaching is a fully remote service. After an initial assessment where I get to know your goals, experience and schedule, I design a personalized training program for you. We then stay in regular contact through weekly check-ins, video reviews and direct messaging. The frequency and format of follow-up can vary — reach out to discuss what fits best for your situation.
Personal training sessions are conducted one-on-one, with full technical supervision throughout. Each session is structured according to a progressive program, and I make real-time corrections and adjustments as we train. Contact me to discuss session frequency, location and format in your specific case.
Every plan starts with understanding you specifically — your goal, your current fitness level, how many days per week you can train, what equipment you have access to and any physical limitations to consider. The program is built from that information, not from a template.
Yes. I offer personalized nutrition planning and guidance within the professional scope corresponding to my practice. This is focused on helping your nutrition support your training and recovery goals in a practical, sustainable way. Get in touch to understand exactly what this includes.
Progress review frequency depends on the service and the arrangement we set up together. For online clients, this typically includes weekly or bi-weekly check-ins.
